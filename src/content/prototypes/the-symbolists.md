## Overview

The Symbolists is a tactical game prototype and Game Design Document I made in 2025. 

The goal was to try to do some serious game design, then make a clean code architecture for grid based combat and navigation.

In this game, you and a team of "Symbolists" fight evil forces on your way to restore world peace by using "Symbols" (which represent ideas or concepts) as a way to cast magic.
It was inspired by Balatro, Into the Breach, and the Mario + Rabbids tactical games.
