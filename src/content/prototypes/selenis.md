## Overview

Selenis is a single player, first-person-view concept I made in 2024 to explore retro-styled cyberpunk art inspired by Lethal Company and Blade Runner.
Pitch : You and a team of scavengers go on missions into the desolate lands outside the city to loot energy cells and sell them at a good price to the black market of Selenis.
I used this concept to work on ShaderGraphs and Post Process Volumes in Unity.
