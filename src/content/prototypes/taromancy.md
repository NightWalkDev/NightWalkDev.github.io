## Overview

Taromancy is a single player card game prototype I made in 2024 to explore card game code architecture.
In this game, you play with cards and use ancient artifacts to channel mana in order to influence the cosmos and prevent the world destruction you've seen in a vision.
It was heavily inspired by Balatro and Inscryption.
