## Overview

Hanamori is a **multiplayer** card game prototype I made in 2023 to learn Unity's online multiplayer workflow.

In this game, you and a strange foreigner play a card game while the sun sets and the sprits of the forest awake from a thousand-year sleep.

It was heavily inspired by **Inscryption** and **Buckshot Roulette**.
