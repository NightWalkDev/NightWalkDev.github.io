## Overview

Out of stock ! is a multiplayer party game in which you and your friends play as supermaket employees. 

I worked on the prototype of the game, which became the demo version of the full game now released on Steam.

My tasks :
- Make responsive **player controllers** : movement, haptics, interactions with other players and level elements
- Make a scalable **interaction system** for level and player interactions
- Implement **character customization** : menus, update character across scenes, etc
